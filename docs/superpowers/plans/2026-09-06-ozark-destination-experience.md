# Ozark Destination Experience

## Goal
Make OzarkRoost feel like a premium Ozarks travel magazine + discovery marketplace: destination-first, visually immersive, mobile-first, and conversion-aware.

## Phase 1 — Homepage discovery
- Strengthen the visual exploration rail around real Ozark destination intent.
- Give travelers clear paths into stays, water, trails, towns, and trip-planning content.
- Keep outbound commercial links behind existing `/out` tracking and approved partner controls.

## Phase 2 — Destination hubs
- Expand flagship destinations into indexable landing experiences.
- Connect each destination to stays, adventures, guides, and relevant affiliate opportunities.
- Surface local businesses/listings without fabricating ownership, partnerships, prices, or availability.

## Phase 3 — Editorial + conversion
- Build high-intent trip guides around Buffalo River, Eureka Springs, Ozark National Forest, lakes, waterfalls, caves, and scenic drives.
- Add strong owner/list-your-business CTAs and founding/free-listing messaging.
- Track discovery-to-click behavior through existing analytics and affiliate tracking.
