# OzarkRoost Free Promotion Sweep

The marketing engine now maintains a rolling queue of legitimate free/organic promotion surfaces and prepares truthful, reusable OzarkRoost copy.

## Guardrails

- No CAPTCHA bypassing.
- No fake accounts or identities.
- No private-data scraping.
- No mass unsolicited posting.
- Automatic publishing should only be added for platforms with an official API and an authorized OzarkRoost account.

## Current tracked surfaces

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Yelp Business
- Tripadvisor owner tools
- Facebook Pages
- Nextdoor Business

The daily marketing campaign refreshes the opportunity queue and generates current submission copy. New legitimate free surfaces can be added to `FREE_SOURCES` in `lib/free-promotion-sweep.js`.
