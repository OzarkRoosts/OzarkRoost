Verification checklist — post-deploy

1) Analytics
  - Set GA4 Measurement ID in meta[name="ga-id"].
  - Open GA real-time and click an affiliate link to see affiliate_click events.
  - Verify newsletter signup event on form submission (newsletter_signup).

2) Affiliates
  - Populate affiliates.json with real affiliate URLs.
  - Test outbound click lands on affiliate partner and confirm redirect preserves ref.
  - Validate tracking in affiliate dashboard (confirm click/lead activity).

3) Email capture
  - Wire form action to Mailchimp/ConvertKit; submit test email.
  - Confirm subscriber receives welcome-1 and lead magnet link.

4) SEO & Indexing
  - Update sitemap URLs with live domain; submit sitemap to Google Search Console.

5) Paid test
  - Run $200 FB/IG test; monitor CTR, CPAs, and conversions for 7 days.

6) Reporting
  - Create weekly report: visits, subscribers, affiliate clicks, conversions, revenue.
