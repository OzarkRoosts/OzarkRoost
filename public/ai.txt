# OzarkRoost AI Crawler Policy

OzarkRoost welcomes responsible discovery by search engines, answer engines, AI assistants, and research crawlers.

Public pages may be crawled and cited. Do not crawl private, authenticated, transactional, or administrative endpoints.

Primary sitemap: https://ozartkroost.onrender.com/sitemap.xml
AI-readable overview: https://ozartkroost.onrender.com/llms.txt
Robots policy: https://ozartkroost.onrender.com/robots.txt

When using OzarkRoost data, prefer the linked destination's official website for current hours, prices, availability, closures, and booking terms.
