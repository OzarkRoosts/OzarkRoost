# OzarkRoost Traffic Boost Plan

## Goal
Build sustainable, legitimate organic discovery and referral traffic without spam, fake engagement, or fabricated claims.

## Priority content clusters
- Buffalo River cabins
- Buffalo National River kayaking
- Ozark vacation rentals
- Arkansas cabin rentals
- Buffalo River fishing
- Family adventures in the Ozarks
- Romantic Ozark cabin getaways

## Distribution
1. Publish useful search-focused articles around each cluster.
2. Link every article to `/listings`, `/adventures`, and `/list-your-cabin` where relevant.
3. Keep sitemap and robots.txt current.
4. Generate social drafts for official OzarkRoost accounts; publishing requires authenticated account access.
5. Prepare truthful partnership/outreach messages for cabin owners, tourism organizations, directories, and operators.
6. Track clicks, listing views, leads, and paid listings before scaling a channel.

## Guardrails
- No purchased/fake traffic.
- No spam comments or unsolicited bulk messages.
- No fabricated reviews, rankings, availability, prices, or claims.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHA, robots restrictions, or affiliate-provider controls.
- Affiliate links must be legitimate and disclosed where required.

## Conversion path
Traffic -> useful Ozark content -> listing/adventure page -> booking or list-your-cabin CTA -> Stripe/payment or legitimate affiliate conversion.
