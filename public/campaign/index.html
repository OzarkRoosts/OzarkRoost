The Thesis: Why We Bench Google

Google's entire model is rent-seeking on intent — you pay them to reach people who were already looking for you. Every algorithm update, every CPC increase, every SERP reshuffle is a toll hike on your own customers.

Roost Nation flips the model. We don't rent attention from Google — we own it. An email list nobody can de-rank. A community nobody can shadow-ban. Content that lives on platforms we control. Revenue from affiliate partnerships and local businesses who pay US directly — not through a middleman.

By the end of this campaign, Ozark Roost isn't a directory. It's the media brand of the Ozarks — the place locals check before they go anywhere, and tourists find before they plan anything. And Google's not in the equation.

Why Google Stays on the Bench

Google Ads: The Problem

You pay $2-15 per click for traffic that vanishes the moment you stop paying. CPCs rise every quarter. Competitors outbid you. Google takes 90% of the value and gives you a click.

AdSense: The Problem

Google keeps ~32% of ad revenue and pays you fractions of a cent per impression. They control what shows on YOUR site. One policy change and your income disappears.

Roost Nation: The Solution

Direct affiliate partnerships pay 5-12% per sale — not pennies per click. You keep 100% of the commission. The link lives on YOUR content. The audience is YOUR list.

Owned Media: The Solution

Email list, SMS list, community members, social followers — these are YOUR assets. No algorithm change, bid increase, or policy update can take them away. You own the relationship.
